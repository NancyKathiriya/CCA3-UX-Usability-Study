## Final Conclusion: Usability Study

Study Overview
This study compared the original Housing.com website with the modified PropNest high-fidelity prototype. Five participants completed real-estate tasks using the Think Aloud method.

Key Findings
- Pricing transparency: Participants appreciated seeing the base price, additional charges, and total cost together.
- Search and filters: Custom budget filtering helped participants find properties matching their requirements.
- Property comparison: The comparison feature reduced the need to switch between multiple browser tabs, although improvements to the comparison functionality are needed.
- Legal information: A visible RERA verification badge made legal information easier to notice.
- Neighbourhood safety and site visits: Safety indicators and a calendar-based site-visit booking flow provided useful information and a clearer process.

Areas for Improvement
The prototype could benefit from larger text, more reliable property comparisons, and continued usability testing to identify additional issues.

Conclusion
Across the five Think Aloud sessions, participants identified useful improvements in pricing transparency, property discovery, comparison, legal verification, and neighbourhood safety. The findings suggest that PropNest addresses several difficulties encountered during the tasks on Housing.com. Further testing and refinement are recommended before final implementation.
